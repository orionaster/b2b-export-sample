OverviewSpecificationsDocumentationRelated productsTechnical questions

Why PFA.

A starting point for high-purity lines where the tube wall needs to be seen and the chemistry is demanding.

Translucent wall

Visual inspection of flow and potential bubbles is possible through the tube wall.

Chemical resistance

PFA is selected for aggressive chemical delivery and high-purity process lines.

Coil or straight

Reference sizes can be supplied in coils or straight lengths for different line layouts.

Wall, bore and outer diameter.

Wall thickness is the difference between outer and inner diameter, divided by two. The diagram uses the reference geometry of this concept.
