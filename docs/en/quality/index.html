QUALITY AND DOCUMENTATION

Documented at every step.

A concept quality process for high-purity tubing, from incoming resin to a labeled, traceable pack.

A simple production flow.

The steps below describe how a production order would be handled. They are not a certification claim.

Verify

Incoming resin and batch records are checked against the approved specification.

Extrude

Tubing runs are monitored for outer diameter, inner diameter and wall thickness.

Inspect

Reference dimensions and appearance are checked before packing.

Pack

Coils and straight lengths are labeled, protected and packed with batch visibility.

Documents a production order can include.

Documentation is confirmed per product and per order.

Certificate of analysis

A batch-level material and test summary, where applicable.

Dimensional report

Measured outer diameter, inner diameter and wall thickness.

Material certificate

Resin grade and batch reference, where available.

Traceability record

A batch label linking the pack to the production record.

Declaration of conformity

A project-specific declaration, confirmed before order.

Packed for the line, not just the pallet.

Coils and straight lengths are labeled, protected and packed so the reference and batch stay visible.

  • Batch and reference label
  • Protective wrap
  • Carton or crate
